Questions

What people ask us first.

The method, the people behind it and how working with us looks in practice.

The method

Is this AI-generated research?

No. Every answer in a Tribu study comes from a real person we recruit for that study. We never replace respondents with simulated or synthetic answers.

AI comes in after the data is collected: a swarm of specialized agents analyzes those answers with several techniques at once, at a depth a single analyst cannot reach in the same time. Our market research experts design the study, train the agents on your category and interpret every result. AI does the depth; people do the judgment.

What exactly is the agent swarm?

A team of specialized AI agents built for each study. An architect agent reads your questions and the questionnaire and sizes the team: from about 15 agents in fast mode to up to 140 in a full study.

Analyst agents attack each key question in parallel, each with a different technique and without seeing each other's work. An arbiter compares what they find. Independent agents recompute every figure, and a devil's-advocate agent tests the alternative explanation. Only then does a finding move on to our experts. Every deck reports how many agents worked on it.

Why several techniques per question instead of one?

Because one method only sees what it was built to see. When two to four techniques reach the same answer independently, you can act on it with confidence.

When they disagree, the gap is often the most valuable finding. A typical example: people say they stopped buying because of price, while the model shows the real drivers were memory and distance to the shelf. One method alone would have given you the wrong answer.

What do your market research experts actually do?

They are behind every step that requires judgment. They frame the business question with you and decide what the study must answer. They design the sample, the questionnaire and the protocol. They train the agents with your category's context and hypotheses.

When the analysis is done, they interpret the findings, decide what matters for your decision and approve the conclusion, the storyline and the final deck. A study goes through four expert approvals before it reaches you.

Can the AI get it wrong? How do you control it?

Any analysis can be wrong, which is why the swarm is built to challenge itself. Techniques are compared against each other, an independent agent that never saw the analysis recalculates each number and its base, and a devil's-advocate agent looks for the alternative explanation. Findings that don't hold are rejected before they reach a slide.

On top of that, nothing leaves Tribu without the sign-off of a senior researcher.

The people we interview

Who are the respondents?

Real buyers of your category, recruited from scratch for each study through targeted digital ads. We define the profile with you (category, brands, frequency, demographics, country), reach those people where they are, and screen them before they enter the study.

Most of them have never answered a study like yours. In our studies, 97% of respondents are real category buyers, against an industry average below 70%.

Why don't you use panels?

Panels reuse the same people study after study. Over time they become professional respondents: they learn what researchers want to hear, answer faster and with less care, and many are not real buyers of the category they are asked about.

Recruiting fresh for every study costs more effort, but gives you better data. And better data is the only way to get deeper insights: the best analysis cannot fix bad answers.

How do you make sure answers are real and honest?

Every respondent goes through screening questions on their actual category behavior before entering the study. During the interview, consistency checks and attention questions flag careless or contradictory answers, and duplicate or suspicious entries are removed before the analysis.

DeepQuery also helps: because it asks follow-up questions, vague or copied answers become evident quickly.

In which countries and categories do you work?

We have run more than 600 projects in 29 countries across Latin America, the US, the UK, Europe and Asia, for 17 industries: food and beverages, personal and home care, financial services, retail, automotive, media and telecom, among others.

Because we recruit through digital ads, we can reach category buyers in almost any market where people are online.

What happens to my data?

Your briefs, your results and your respondents' answers are used only for your study and are treated as confidential. We work under NDA when needed, and respondents' personal data is protected and never sold or shared.

Working with Tribu

What kind of questions can you answer?

If it matters to your business, we can research it. We cover the whole brand cycle: discovering what is going on and why (category landscape, consumer deep dives, segmentation), testing what works (concepts, claims, packs, pricing, brand drivers) and tracking how the market moves (brand health, trends, forecasting).

We don't sell packaged products. Every program starts with your question; then we choose the technology and the protocol that fit it.

How fast are results?

Days, not weeks. We are about three times faster than a traditional agency because recruitment, technology and analysis are all in-house: there are no handoffs between a panel provider, a fieldwork house, a tabulation team and the analysts, and the agents work in parallel.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope: number of markets, sample size and depth of the analysis. Because we are fully integrated, a Tribu study typically costs 30% to 50% less than an equivalent traditional study. The best way to see the value first is the free pilot.

What do I receive at the end?

A decision-ready deck where every slide states one finding, in plain language, with the people and techniques behind it. Where it helps, live simulators your team can use (for example, to test prices or product configurations). And an annex with the full method, including how many agents worked on your study.

A Tribu strategist presents the results to your team and stays available for follow-up questions.

Can you work with our existing data or trackers?

Yes. We can combine new data with your trackers, past studies and market data. The value is often in the relationship between sources: every variable is analyzed against the rest, which opens up more complex questions.

What do I get in the free pilot?

One business question, answered in one week, at no cost and with no commitment. We recruit real category buyers for your question, run the agent swarm and deliver a short deck with the findings and the method behind them, presented by one of our experts.

It is the best way to judge the depth of our work with your own question.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Tribu collects, why, and what rights you have. It covers three groups of people: visitors to tribudata.com, the clients and prospects who contact us, and the people who take part in our studies (respondents).

1. Who we are

Tribu Data, Buenos Aires, Argentina, is the data controller for the processing described here. For anything related to your personal data, write to simon@tribudata.com.

2. Data we collect

Website visitors. Technical data (IP address, browser, device, pages viewed) and interaction events (clicks on the free pilot button, video plays, form starts), collected through our analytics tool and the cookies described in section 8.

Prospects and clients. What you send through our forms or by email (name, company, role, work email, country and your business question), contract and billing details, and the files and datasets you share with us for a study.

Respondents. Your answers in our surveys and interviews, your screening answers (category purchase, profile) and basic technical data from the device you answer on. We recruit through digital ads; the ad platform's own processing is governed by its privacy policy. We ask for contact details only to administer an incentive or a follow-up interview, and we keep them separate from your answers.

3. Why we use it

To run studies and deliver findings to our clients (performance of a contract with clients; consent of respondents). To answer your enquiry and run the free pilot (steps prior to a contract and our legitimate interest in business communication). To screen respondents and keep data quality: detecting duplicate, careless or fraudulent answers (legitimate interest). To improve our methods and tools, using aggregated or anonymised data (legitimate interest). To measure and improve the website (consent where cookies require it). To comply with legal obligations.

4. How we use AI

Our analysis is done by software agents under the supervision of Tribu's researchers. Before analysis, respondent data is pseudonymised: direct identifiers are removed or replaced by codes. Data from a study is used only for that study and never to train models for other clients or third parties. Where we use external AI providers, we do so under contractual terms that prohibit them from using the data to train their models.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (cloud hosting and storage, survey and interview platforms, ad platforms for recruitment, analytics, AI providers, invoicing), under contracts that limit what they may do with it; with our clients, who receive findings in aggregated form and verbatims without identifying details, never a respondent's identity; and with authorities, when the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Tribu operates from Argentina and runs studies in Latin America, the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe and Asia, so data may be processed on servers outside your country. When data of people in the EU or the UK is transferred, we rely on standard contractual clauses or on an adequacy decision; Argentina holds an EU adequacy decision.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries and pilot requests: 12 months after the last contact, unless a project follows. Client project data: for the duration of the contract and three years afterwards, then deleted or anonymised, or earlier if the client asks. Respondent answers: pseudonymised for analysis and kept for 12 months after the study is delivered, so that every number can be re-run and audited; contact details for incentives are deleted within 30 days of payment. Website analytics: 13 months.

8. Cookies

tribudata.com uses strictly necessary cookies and, with your consent, analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Respondents can leave a study at any point. To exercise any right, write to simon@tribudata.com; we answer within 10 business days. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in Argentina, the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP); in the EU, your national authority; in the UK, the ICO.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to the people working on a study, and every step of our analysis runs as code, so it can be audited and reproduced. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Minors

Our website and our studies are intended for adults. We survey people under 18 only when a study requires it and with the consent of a parent or guardian.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version. Material changes are announced on the site.
